Assumptions & methodology

Return convention
The selected annual return is treated as an effective annual total return and converted to an equivalent monthly rate: (1 + annual return)^(1/12) − 1.
Option 1 — Maximum Income
The default monthly distribution equals initial capital multiplied by the equivalent monthly return. Under a constant-return assumption this is designed to keep nominal capital approximately unchanged. It does not preserve purchasing power when inflation is positive.
Option 2 — Preserve Purchasing Power
The simulator solves for an inflation-linked starting monthly distribution while targeting ending nominal capital equal to initial capital compounded by the selected inflation rate. This aims to preserve the original capital's purchasing power.
Option 3 — Balanced
The default first-year distribution is 2.4% of initial capital annually and rises with inflation. The remaining return stays invested. Nominal capital may grow while purchasing power can still decline.
MGS/GII cash-flow warning
MGS/GII yield-to-maturity is not the same as coupon/profit cash income. Government securities normally make periodic payments rather than monthly distributions. A real implementation may require a cash reserve or maturity/coupon ladder to smooth spending into monthly cash flow.
