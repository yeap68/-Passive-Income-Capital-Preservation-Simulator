6. Assumptions & methodology

How the simulator calculates returns

The selected annual portfolio return is treated as an effective annual total return and converted to an equivalent monthly rate. Portfolio growth is credited monthly before that month's distribution is deducted.

For example, under Maximum Income, the default monthly distribution is calculated from the equivalent monthly return rather than simply dividing the annual percentage by twelve. This is why RM5 million at a 4.17% effective annual return produces approximately RM17,051.51 per month under this model while maintaining approximately RM5 million of nominal capital.

Inflation treatment

Inflation compounds annually. When inflation-linked distributions are selected, the monthly distribution increases once each year by the selected inflation rate.

Real capital is the projected nominal balance divided by cumulative inflation. It therefore represents approximate purchasing power in today's ringgit.

MGS / GII implementation note

This simulator models portfolio total return and monthly distributions. Actual Malaysian Government Securities and Government Investment Issues normally make coupon or profit payments semiannually rather than monthly. A practical income portfolio therefore requires cash-flow management, such as a liquidity reserve or maturity ladder, to create smooth monthly spending.

Yield to maturity is also not identical to coupon or profit cash flow. Purchase price, accrued interest, premium or discount to par, maturity value, reinvestment rates and dealing spreads can affect realised results.

Important limitations

Results are mathematical illustrations, not guaranteed investment outcomes. The model assumes the selected return and inflation rates remain constant. It does not model taxes, transaction costs, bid-offer spreads, default events, changes in reinvestment yields or early-sale market-price risk.

7. Reference points

Point-in-time reference used in this simulator: Malaysia 10-year MGS benchmark yield approximately 4.17% on 15 September 2026. The 3.00% inflation setting is a planning assumption rather than a forecast.
